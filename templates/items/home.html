How CampusCare Works

Simple, safe, and transparent 3-step lost & found process

1. Report Details

Fill out item title, location, date, and upload a clear picture. Choose whether your phone/email is shared or routed via Campus Desk.

2. Search & Verify

Filter by building, category, and date. Campus authentication ensures that only verified students and staff access contact info.

3. Reconnect & Reward

Meet safely at the campus helpdesk or agreed spot. Mark the item resolved and earn reward recognition for campus citizenship!
